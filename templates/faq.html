Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how Taskmill works

Platform Operating Hours

What are the optimization service hours? +

Optimization services operate daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM (EST).

When is customer support available? +

Customer support and maintenance assistance are available between 10:00 AM and 10:00 PM (EST).

Product Optimization Policy

Do I need to confirm project details before optimization? +

Yes. Clients must confirm objectives and project scope before any optimization work begins.

How are optimization results determined? +

Results depend on performance metrics, product readiness, and data quality provided by the client.

What performance improvement should I expect? +

Typical improvements range from 10% to 200%, depending on product type and optimization scope.

Account & Security

How do I keep my account secure? +

Always keep login credentials confidential and use unique passwords.

What happens after failed login attempts? +

After three incorrect login attempts, accounts are temporarily locked for security reasons.

Payments & Requests

How do I verify a payment? +

Always confirm payment details with official Taskmill support and provide transaction proof.

When does project execution begin? +

Execution starts after payment verification and project briefing confirmation.

Client Tiers

How are client tiers determined? +

Client tiers depend on subscription level, project scale, and engagement history.

What benefits do higher tiers receive? +

Higher tiers receive faster responses, dedicated specialists, and exclusive optimization tools.

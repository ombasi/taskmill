Help centre

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about tasks, wallet, membership, and withdrawals.

What are the operating hours?
Optimization and support typically run daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Chat and tickets outside those hours are answered as soon as the team is online.
How do I start earning?
Register, fund your wallet (keep at least {{ reserve_txt }}), complete product review tasks in your daily sets, then withdraw when the checklist is green.
Why was money held from my balance?
When a task is assigned, the product price is held from your balance. When you submit the review, the price is returned plus your commission.
What is a combo / negative balance?
A high-value product may take your balance negative. Deposit enough to clear the hold to zero or above, then submit the product to unlock combo commission.
How do withdrawals work?
Add a payout method once (admin must confirm it). Request a withdrawal while leaving at least {{ reserve_txt }} as reserve for the next day’s tasks. Enter your withdraw PIN. Admin reviews and pays out to your confirmed method. Amounts are shown in your local currency.
How do referrals work?
Share your referral code. When a referred member activates and deposits according to platform rules, you may earn a referral bonus credited to your wallet.
I forgot my password or withdraw PIN
Contact support or use live chat. An admin can reset your login password or withdraw PIN. After a password reset you will be asked to set a new one.
